Radiologic Interpretation Guide

Warm colors (Crimson / Orange / Yellow) designate anatomical regions where convolutional feature maps in the final dense block contributed highest gradient activation towards the prediction. For Pneumonia, these typically localize to alveolar consolidations, bronchovascular bundle thickening, or diffuse interstitial infiltrates. Cool colors (Deep Blue) denote non-contributory background or normal aerated lung parenchyma.
